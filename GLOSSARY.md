# Course Purchases

## Language

**Purchase cancellation**:
The cancellation of a customer's course purchase following approval of their request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

**Purchase cancellation request**:
A customer's request to cancel a course purchase, awaiting review until approved or declined. A request does not itself mean the purchase has been cancelled or money returned.
_Avoid_: Refund request, reversal request, chargeback

**Refund**:
Money returned to a customer following approval of a purchase cancellation request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback
